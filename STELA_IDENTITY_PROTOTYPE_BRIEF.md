# Stela Identity Prototype Brief

Status: Round 1 approved direction  
Scope: Form-only comparison of six identity systems  
Families: Modern Epigraphy and Refined Lozenge

> Superseding application decision, 23 July 2026: the permanent physical engraving contains only the unique identifier. The Stela logo, QR code, and optional web address belong to a separate transparent companion label. References below to a logo-bearing physical asset signature or engraving performance are retained only as Round 1 history and are no longer production requirements.

## Objective

Develop a primary `STELA` inscription and a secondary symbol that together express permanent physical identification, controlled inscription, and a record that remains connected to the asset.

The inscription is the primary identity. The symbol accompanies it when space, recognition, or the companion label requires it.

## Brand Meaning

Stela is named after an inscribed monument whose information belongs to its physical surface and is intended to remain legible across time.

The identity should translate the principles of a stela rather than depict an ancient monument:

- information physically inscribed into a surface;
- permanence through material presence;
- deliberate placement and alignment;
- stable, upright proportions;
- an inscription revealed by directional light;
- a record inseparable from the object that carries it.

## Required Character

- precise;
- permanent;
- established;
- modern;
- trustworthy;
- materially credible;
- restrained;
- legible;
- ownable.

## Must Not Resemble

- a cryptocurrency or blockchain brand;
- a cybersecurity shield;
- a navigation arrow or cursor;
- a decorative badge or jewel;
- a gravestone, doorway, book, or museum emblem;
- an archaeological or classical-luxury brand;
- a generic industrial supplier;
- a futuristic interface icon.

## Prototype Families

### Modern Epigraphy

**E1 - Inscribed Plane**  
An upright surface carrying one precise incision and origin point. The wordmark uses controlled terminal cuts derived from the same gesture.

**E2 - Registered Inscription**  
A lighter, more open system organized around inscription lines, a registration datum, and one deliberate interruption.

**E3 - Monolithic Letterform**  
A stronger wordmark and compact solid-plane symbol. It tests whether greater mass can communicate permanence without becoming industrial or heavy.

### Refined Lozenge

**L1 - Balanced Origin**  
The strongest previous concept reduced to complete geometry, one inner contour, a small origin point, and a short trace.

**L2 - Open Incision**  
A less emblematic lozenge whose perimeter opens only where the controlled incision begins.

**L3 - Inscribed Lozenge**  
A bridge between both families: the lozenge acts as an inscription field rather than a decorative container.

## Round 1 Constraints

- Black and white only.
- No gradients, shadows, glare, texture, or photography.
- No explanatory concept names in the first unaided review.
- Every system must show inscription, symbol, and combined lockup.
- Every system receives identical scale, spacing, and application tests.
- Symbols must remain clear at 16, 20, and 24 pixels.
- The combined lockup must remain legible in a compact navbar.
- The transparent companion label must work in one color beside a machine-readable code.
- Fine lines must not be the only source of recognition.

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Communicates inscription and permanence | 25% |
| Distinctive and recognizably Stela | 20% |
| Strength of the inscription | 20% |
| Small-scale and label-print performance | 20% |
| Premium trust and credibility | 15% |

## Rejection Flags

A candidate should not advance if repeated reviewers identify it as:

- arrow-like;
- badge-like;
- crypto-like;
- funerary;
- ornamental;
- generic;
- fragile;
- illegible;
- overly industrial;
- dependent on an explanation.

## Review Method

1. Present candidates in a neutral order using only their codes.
2. Give reviewers five seconds per primary lockup.
3. Ask for three unaided words and any immediate association.
4. Show symbol, inscription, small-scale, and asset-signature tests.
5. Score each criterion from 1 to 5.
6. Select two candidates for deeper review without group discussion.
7. Collect responses before revealing the concept families.
8. Advance the strongest candidate from each family unless one fails a mandatory production test.

## Round 1 Deliverables

- Six normalized identity-system prototypes.
- Inscription-only specimen for each candidate.
- Symbol-only specimen for each candidate.
- Combined lockup for each candidate.
- 16, 20, and 24 pixel symbol tests.
- Single-color transparent-label specimen.
- Reviewer scoring sheet.
