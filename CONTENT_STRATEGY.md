# Stela Content Strategy

## Role of the Website

The homepage is the main presentation of Stela. Supporting pages deepen understanding only where a visitor has a specific product, application, collaboration, investor, or contact question. The site should remain concise enough to encourage a direct conversation rather than become a documentation portal.

## Public Information

The public site may explain:

- the physical-origin and digital-proof concept;
- the intended controlled marking and evidence workflow;
- potential application areas;
- the current pre-commercial development stage;
- categories of potential collaboration;
- the high-level intended business model;
- known limitations and areas still subject to validation.

## Private Information

The following should remain available only through a qualified direct conversation or private brief:

- detailed product and system architecture;
- technical specifications and validation evidence;
- financial projections and detailed commercial assumptions;
- sensitive market research and competitive analysis;
- detailed pilot plans and partner-specific workflows;
- IP strategy and filing details;
- current risks, milestones, founder plan, and operating plan.

## Pre-commercial Claim Rules

Stela is in pre-commercial development and validation. Public copy must not imply active commercial deployment, completed technical validation, granted patents, established major partnerships, proven market demand, guaranteed outcomes, or verified projections.

Use language such as `designed to`, `intended to`, `potential`, `being investigated`, `under development`, `subject to validation`, `pilot discussion`, and `validation partner` where maturity is not established.

Concrete claims about historical permanent-marking applications, completed milestones, technical performance, customer demand, market size, IP status, or partner relationships require founder confirmation and supporting evidence before publication.

## Editorial Standard

- Keep headings short and confident.
- Use concise paragraphs and restrained lists.
- Do not repeat the homepage in longer form.
- Let each page answer one visitor question.
- End every supporting page with one clear next step.
- Prefer honest specificity over broad claims.
