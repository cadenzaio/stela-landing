# Stela Logo And Hero Exploration

This iteration focuses only on the identity-defining system: the mark and the hero.

## Logo Directions

1. Etched lozenge
   A fine diamond outline with an inner offset line. It feels markable and premium, but can become generic if it has no point of origin.

2. Refraction trace
   Two crossed light lines with no enclosing shape. It is elegant and glass-like, but too fragile at navbar scale.

3. Origin incision
   A small origin point held inside a fine lozenge, crossed by a restrained incision line. This best connects physical mark, surface, and proof without becoming an arrow.

4. Precision line mark
   A single horizontal cut with a small verification point. It is very quiet and engravable, but less ownable as a logo.

5. Physical-digital bridge
   Two small fields connected by one line. It explains the concept clearly, but risks reading as a UI diagram instead of a brand mark.

## Hero Directions

1. Surface first
   A large dark glass material plane holds the permanent mark. The certificate is secondary and linked by a fine proof line.

2. Certificate first
   A polished proof card dominates the visual. This is clear but too SaaS and too digital for Stela.

3. Macro incision
   The hero becomes a close-up of a mark being cut into material. Strong physicality, but it can feel industrial if pushed too far.

4. Split provenance
   Physical surface and proof record sit as two balanced objects. Conceptually clear, but less iconic than making the surface the origin.

## Recommended Combined Direction

Use origin incision + surface first.

The brand mark becomes a fine lozenge with a small origin point and a refracted incision line. The hero makes the same language physical: a dark glass-like marked surface is the emotional center, with a restrained proof card attached as evidence rather than as the main object.

This direction is the strongest Stela standard because it is premium, engravable, quiet, physical, and specific to the promise: a permanent mark becomes the origin of a trusted digital record.

## V6 Refinement Variants

The live defaults are `LOGO_VARIANT = "origin-lozenge"` and `HERO_VARIANT = "etched-origin"` in `components/landing/StelaLanding.tsx`.

### Logo Variants

1. Origin Lozenge
   The recommended default. It uses a brighter primary lozenge, softer inner contour, crisp origin point, and short incision/proof line. It is the clearest continuation of the current mark and the most engravable.

2. Open Incision
   A related mark with intentional openings in the contour. It feels more reflective and lighter, while retaining the origin point and physical cut language.

3. Refracted Origin
   Offset angular contours with the origin point held between them. It is the most optical variant and useful if the brand should lean more glass/refraction than engraving.

### Hero Variants

1. Etched Origin
   The recommended default. It makes the cut line, origin point, and physical surface the primary focus, while reducing the proof card to attached evidence.

2. Refracted Surface
   A slightly more ethereal version with stronger surface reflection and optical refraction. It remains physical, but feels more glass-like.

3. Proof Emergence
   A variant where the proof line and certificate emergence are clearer. The mark still leads, but the physical-to-digital transformation is more explicit.
